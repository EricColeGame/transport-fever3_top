## Strategic Tips for Network Optimization

To hit the ground running once you gain access, keeping these tactical rules in mind will ensure your transport balance sheet stays solidly in the black:

1. **Avoid Empty Backhauls**: When running long-distance freight trains, design multi-stop loops where your rolling stock carries raw timber outward and brings finished goods or construction materials on the return leg.
2. **Prioritize Lane Connections Early**: Utilize the new intersection management tools to create dedicated right-turn bypasses and bus-only lanes before city centers develop chronic traffic jams.
3. **Establish Staging Depots**: Do not force heavy freight trucks into dense downtown alleyways. Build perimeter warehouses on town outskirts and use clean electric delivery vans or cargo trams for last-mile drop-offs.
4. **Monitor Citizen Noise Sensitivity**: Keep heavy industrial railways away from booming residential districts to maintain high land value and maximize population growth rates.
