## Smarter Logistics: Redesigned Cargo and Offshore Industries

A major takeaway from **transport fever 3 what we know** is the overhaul of supply chain management. In past games, industries operated on rigid delivery lines: if an industry did not detect an unbroken transport chain linking it to an end consumer, it simply refused to produce cargo.

The new model introduces demand-driven production boosted by workforce presence and optional secondary inputs. Over 35 distinct industrial facilities populate the map, requiring multifaceted logistics pipelines to achieve maximum production capacity.

| Supply Chain Component | Functional Role | Operational Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Offshore Platforms** | Deep-sea oil extraction & fishing grounds | Expands resource collection into open maritime zones |
| **Intermediate Warehouses** | Central storage depots for varied freight | Buffers seasonal supply shocks and decouples routes |
| **Specialized Terminals** | Customized loading points for single goods | Prevents mixed-cargo bottlenecks at central rail hubs |
| **Worker Transit Lines** | Commuter routes shuttling town inhabitants | Directly boosts production rates at connected facilities |

Based on **transport fever 3 what we know** about infrastructure tools, intermediate warehouses fundamentally change freight network design. Players can build consolidated regional logistics parks, running heavy freight trains from outlying production facilities into a central depot. From there, fleets of light haulers or cargo trams distribute crates across local urban markets without clogging regional train terminals.

## Advanced Road Layouts and Traffic Lane Control

Traffic congestion has always challenged route efficiency once dynamic towns expand into large cities. Private personal vehicles frequently clogged trunk avenues, delaying scheduled bus lines and stalling freight trucks. 

To resolve these issues, the game provides comprehensive intersection design options and granular lane control tools. You can customize individual lane designations, determine specific turning priorities at intersections, and build dedicated overland tramways that run completely independent of street traffic.

### Engineering Highway Ramps and Junctions

Highway construction is no longer constrained by rigid, snapping roadway segments. The newly developed toolset enables fluid highway on-ramps, sweeping multi-lane interchanges, and custom bypass loops designed to channel cross-country freight around crowded residential cores.

Vehicles also exhibit realistic passing and overtaking behavior. Faster commuter cars no longer queue patiently behind slow-moving industrial machinery on two-lane roads; instead, they pass when opposing lanes are clear, vastly improving regional throughput.

| Traffic Control Tool | Practical Application | Impact on Network Throughput |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Configurable Lane Connections** | Dictate which approach lane maps to turning lanes | Eliminates weaving conflicts at busy downtown intersections |
| **Dedicated Transit Lanes** | Isolate bus and tram traffic from commuter cars | Protects passenger route schedules from urban gridlock |
| **Highway Split/Merge Ramps** | Construct high-speed ingress and egress lanes | Keeps intercity through-traffic moving at top speed |
| **Data Diagnostic Overlays** | Highlight noise, pollution, and traffic hot spots | Pinpoints localized issues before they harm urban growth |

To inspect system health, players can access real-time data overlays. These heatmaps pinpoint emission spikes, sound pollution zones, and transit delays, allowing engineers to isolate problem corridors before civic dissatisfaction halts town development.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the biggest new features confirmed for Transport Fever 3?
The sequel introduces over 275 vehicles, light rail transit, dedicated cargo trams, offshore industries like oil rigs and fisheries, intermediate warehouses, advanced lane-connection tools, a subarctic climate zone, dynamic weather, and a complete day-night cycle.

### How does the cargo system differ from earlier games?
Unlike older systems that relied on rigid, end-to-end consumer demand, the new simulation features demand-driven industrial expansion boosted by worker transit and secondary inputs. Dedicated intermediate warehouses also allow players to store, sort, and transfer goods across regional hubs.

### In Transport Fever 3 what we know so far, will there be mod support at launch?
Yes, mod support is central to the design. The game includes an upgraded in-game map editor, built-in modification tools, and an integrated in-game mod browser for downloading player-made vehicles, buildings, and maps seamlessly.

### Can you play the game as a casual sandbox builder?
Yes. The title features an extensive tycoon mode with adjustable difficulty settings. Players who want a stress-free experience can disable the financial simulation entirely to build sprawling model railways and detailed urban landscapes without budget restrictions.
