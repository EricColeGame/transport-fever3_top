## Top Construction and Logistics Strategies for PS5 Players

Building sprawling freight networks using a gamepad is an art form. Mastering the newly added track and road construction tools will save you millions in demolition costs and optimize vehicle turnaround times across all eras.

| Phase | Strategic Goal | Implementation Tip on Console |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Era 1: 1850–1900** | Lay the foundation with bulk freight | Connect coal and iron to steel mills via single-track rail with passing sidings. |
| **Era 2: 1900–1950** | Establish intercity passenger corridors | Introduce early passenger steam lines alongside dedicated light rail urban loops. |
| **Era 3: 1950–1990** | Industrial specialization & highway links | Construct highway ramps and bypass roads to divert cargo lorries away from city centers. |
| **Era 4: 1990–Modern** | High-speed transit & multimodal hubs | Upgrade trunk lines with electrified dual-track rail; deploy cargo aircraft for luxury goods. |

Follow these concrete tips when laying down infrastructure:

- **Isolate Cargo Tramways**: When serving downtown commercial zones, build dedicated tram tracks down the road median. This keeps cargo trams out of personal automobile traffic, ensuring high delivery throughput.
- **Utilize Lane Direction Control**: Set custom lane directions at highway off-ramps to prevent delivery trucks from bottlenecking major suburban thoroughfares.
- **Stagger Depot Dispatches**: When deploying a fleet of ten regional buses, do not release them all at once. Pause the simulation, space out departure intervals, and unpause to avoid vehicle bunching.
- **Monitor Citizen Commuting Habits**: Inspect commuter pathfinding overlays before dropping expensive subway stations. Place terminals within a short walking radius of dense residential zones.

By designing your layouts around modular stations that can expand with additional tracks and cargo platforms later, your transit backbone will seamlessly adapt across decades of technological change.
