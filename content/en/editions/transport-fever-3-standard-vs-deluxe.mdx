## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which edition is better in the Transport Fever 3 Standard vs Deluxe matchup?
The Deluxe Edition offers the best overall experience for dedicated simulation fans thanks to its bonus historical vehicle catalog, custom scenarios, and bundled digital soundtrack. However, if you are new to the series or watch your budget closely, the Standard Edition provides the identical core physics engine, career campaign, and procedural sandbox at a lower price point.

### Do I get the pre-order bonuses if I purchase the Standard Edition?
Yes. As long as you pre-order the game prior to its launch, both the Standard Edition and Deluxe Edition include the TS Queen Mary ocean liner, the Great Pyramid of Giza landmark, and the Asian Elephant wildlife addition.

### How does the 10% loyalty discount work for existing players?
If you own *Transport Fever 2* on Steam, GOG, Epic Games Store, PlayStation 5, or Xbox Series X|S, you qualify for an automatic 10% discount on either the Standard Edition or Deluxe Edition of *Transport Fever 3*. To claim this discount, you must purchase the new game on the exact same platform account before the promotion ends 14 days after release.

### Can I upgrade from Standard to Deluxe after launch?
While Urban Games has not published a separate upgrade listing yet, previous installments allowed players to purchase a Deluxe Upgrade Pack post-launch. However, buying the Deluxe Edition upfront or using the 10% loyalty discount during the launch window is generally cheaper than purchasing an upgrade later on.
