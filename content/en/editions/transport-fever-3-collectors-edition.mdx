## Practical Tips for Displaying and Preserving Your Collectibles

Once your box arrives, keeping your physical components in mint condition will preserve their aesthetic and resale value for years to come. Many collectors regret leaving delicate 3D prints or paper collectibles exposed to direct environmental hazards.

### Maintaining the Hero Statue

The 13cm Hero Statue features an individually hand-dappled finish over 3D-printed media, anchored by a wooden pedestal. Because real wood reacts to moisture and dry heat, follow these display best practices:

*   **Avoid Direct Sunlight:** Prolonged ultraviolet exposure can fade the hand-applied paint finish and dry out the natural wood base.
*   **Keep Away From High Humidity:** Do not display the statue in rooms subject to heavy moisture fluctuations, which can cause natural wood to warp or loosen the bond with the fabric base.
*   **Gentle Cleaning Only:** Clean dust buildup using a soft makeup brush or photographic lens blower. Avoid chemical solvents, alcohol wipes, or abrasive cloths that could strip the delicate paint coat.

### Preserving the Hardcover Artbook and Card Deck

Books and printed cards represent historical records of the simulation's design process. Store the 200-page book flat or upright supported by bookends to prevent spine lean over time. If you plan to play the transit card game regularly during tabletop sessions, consider placing the individual cards into standard protective plastic card sleeves to prevent edge fraying and surface scuffs.
